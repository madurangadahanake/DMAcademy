VOCAL DISCIPLINES

ඔබගේ ගායනා අභිලාෂයන්ට අනුව සුදුසුම පාඨමාලා අංශය තෝරාගන්න

North Indian Classical (ශාස්ත්‍රීය ගායනය)

Pure Raga Standard & Swara Sadhana
  • Kharaj Sadhana (පහළ ස්වර සාධනය) සහ Pitch Stabilization.
  • Saptak, Thaat, Alankar සහ Swara Pattern Practice.
  • Raga Development (Alap, Bandish, Taan, Bol-taan).
  • Thal Control (Teental, Dadra, Keharwa, Roopak etc.).
  • Bhatkhande Examination Board Syllabus (Prarambhik to Visharad).
Enroll Classical Vocal

Semi-Classical & Light (සුගම ගී)

Ghazal, Bhajan, Thumri & Melody
  • Ghazal, Bhajan, Bhavgeet සහ Light Classical Articulation.
  • Emotion Expression සහ Lyric Diction (ශබ්ද උච්චාරණය).
  • Murki, Khatka, Gamak සහ Meend Ornamentation.
  • Microphone Technique සහ Studio Recording Singing Skills.
  • Sinhala Classical Song Performance Training.

Voice Culture & Breath Work

Vocal Health, Range & Power
  • Diaphragmatic Breathing සහ Breath Support Techniques.
  • Vocal Range Expansion (Chest Voice, Head Voice, Mixed Voice).
  • Vocal Stamina, Pitch Correction සහ Tone Modulation.
  • Straining නැතිව උස් ස්වර (High Notes) නිවැරදිව ගායනා කිරීම.
  • Vocal Health Care සහ Stage Performance Confidence.
