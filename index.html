Instruments & Courses | උගන්වන වාද්‍ය භාණ්ඩ

දේශීය, පෙරදිග සහ අපරදිග සංගීත භාණ්ඩ රැසක් පුහුණු කෙරේ

String - Local & Eastern

  • Sitar (සිතාරය)
  • Esraj (එස්රාජ්)
  • Veena (වීණාව)
  • Dilruba (දිල්රුබා)
  • Sarod (සරෝද්)
  • Santour (සන්තූර්)
  • Tanpura / Tambura (තම්පුරාව)

String - Western

  • Violin (වයලීනය)
  • Cello (සෙලෝ)
  • Double Bass
  • Acoustic / Classical / Electric Guitar
  • Bass Guitar
  • Ukulele
  • Mandolin & Banjo

Keyboards & Pianos

  • Harmonium / Seraphina (හාමෝනියම්)
  • Piano (පියානෝ)
  • Digital Keyboard / Synthesizer
  • Melodica (මෙලෝඩිකා)
  • Accordion (ඇකෝඩියන්)

Sri Lankan Traditional Drums

  • Geta Beraya (ගැට බෙරය)
  • Yak Beraya / Low Country Drum
  • Daula (දවුල) & Thammattama
  • Udekkiya & Bummadiya
  • Hand Rabana / Virudu Rabana
  • Banku Rabana / Demala Beraya

Eastern & Western Percussion

  • Tabla (තබ්ලාව) & Dholak / Dhol
  • Mridangam, Kanjira, Ghatam
  • Drum Kit & Octapad
  • Conga & Bongo Drums, Cajon
  • Thalampota, Kiri Kodi, Tambourine, Shakers, Triangle

Wind - Local & Eastern

  • Horanawa (හොරණෑව)
  • Bamboo Flute (බුංගලාව / බංසුරි)
  • Pullahangilawa (පුල්ලාන්ගිලාව)
  • Shehnai & Hakgediya
  • Vas Dandu (වස් දඬුව)

Wind - Western

  • Harmonica / Mouth Organ
  • Saxophone (සැක්සෆෝන්)
  • Trumpet & Trombone
  • Clarinet, Flute, Recorder
